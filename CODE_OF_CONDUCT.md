# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, critique work rather than people, and
make room for people of every background, experience level, and language — contributions in English or
Spanish are both welcome.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language or imagery, and
publishing anyone's private information without consent.

## Reporting

Report a concern privately to the maintainer, [@JuanColoradoV](https://github.com/JuanColoradoV),
through a [private report on this repository](../../security/advisories/new) (it is visible only to the
maintainer) or through [GitHub's abuse reporting](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject contributions that don't
follow this code, and may block people who repeatedly or seriously violate it.
